01 | FOUNDATION

Pravakya Foundation
Art • Education • Service

Pravakya Foundation is an initiative built around three interconnected pillars - Art, Education, and Service. We believe that individuals and society grow when creativity is encouraged, learning is practical, and people are given meaningful opportunities to participate and contribute.

Through Art

Creativity, Theatre & Self-Discovery

We promote creativity, cultural awareness, theatrical expression, and communication, creating platforms to understand our cultural roots and nurture human empathy.

“Art is not only about performance. It is a way of understanding ourselves, our surroundings, and one another.”
Conducted: Mayadveepa
Through Education

Practical Learning & Life Skills

We focus on career readiness, financial literacy, communication, and professional development, helping students connect classroom knowledge with real-world practice.

“Education should not stop with textbooks. Learning becomes meaningful when connected with lived situations.”
Conducted: Fun with Financials
Through Service

Social Engagement & Empowerment

We encourage youth civic responsibility, environmental awareness, and community initiatives, inspiring individuals to use their skills for the betterment of society.

“Service is about understanding that our knowledge and abilities can be used for the benefit of others.”
Initiatives In Development

Pravakya Foundation works with people, institutions, artists, educators, and professionals to create spaces where everyone can learn, explore, participate, share their skills, and contribute.

Our aim is to bring these three dimensions together through meaningful programs and experiences that inspire creativity, empower learning, and create a positive impact in society.

ART & CULTURE · THEATRE PRODUCTION

Mayadveepa - The Magical Island

20 Student Performers · 30-Minute Play
Theatre Project

Exploring Storytelling, Teamwork & Stage Performance

Mayadveepa is a theatre project by Pravakya Foundation that brought together 20 students from primary to high school to create and perform a 30-minute stage production. The project was developed in collaboration with Navachethana School, Venur, giving young students an opportunity to explore storytelling, acting, creativity, teamwork, and stage performance.

“More than just a play, Mayadveepa was an opportunity for young minds to express themselves, discover their abilities, work together, and experience the joy of theatre.”
Production Credits & Details

Our Story

Pravakya began with a simple thought - people need spaces where they can discover themselves, learn from experience, express their abilities, and contribute to society.

The idea grew from experiences in theatre, education, professional life, and working with people from different backgrounds. Over time, it became clear that art and creativity can help people discover confidence and perspective; education can help them understand opportunities and prepare for life; and service can help them use what they have learned for something beyond themselves.

This led to the evolution of Pravakya Foundation around three pillars: Art. Education. Service.

Founder

Founder’s Message

Josith Shetty

Founder, Pravakya Foundation

I believe that meaningful change begins when people are given the opportunity to express themselves, learn beyond boundaries, and contribute to the society around them. My experiences in art, theatre, education, and professional life made me realise that creativity and knowledge become truly valuable when they are transformed into meaningful action.

This belief led to the evolution of Pravakya Foundation into a platform that brings together Art, Education, and Service. Through our initiatives, we aim to create spaces where people can explore their creativity, develop practical knowledge and life skills, discover opportunities.

Pravakya is envisioned as a space where artists, educators, students, professionals, and institutions can come together, share their abilities, learn from one another, and create a positive impact in society.

- Josith Shetty | Founder, Pravakya Foundation

“
Co-Founder’s Message

Shrikantta Rao

Co-Founder, Pravakya Foundation

I believe that art has the power to connect people, education has the power to open possibilities, and service gives us the responsibility to use what we learn for the betterment of society. These three dimensions form the foundation of Pravakya.

Our journey is about creating meaningful opportunities for people to learn, create, participate, and contribute. Through cultural experiences, practical learning, creative initiatives, and social engagement, we want Pravakya to become a platform where individuals can discover their abilities and use them with purpose.

We envision a society where creativity is encouraged, learning is practical, and service becomes a shared responsibility.
