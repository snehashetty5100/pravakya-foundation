Who We Are

A Purpose-Driven Platform
for Learning, Culture & Service

Pravakya Foundation is a purpose-driven initiative working at the intersection of Art, Education and Service. We believe that education should go beyond classrooms, art should go beyond stages, and service should go beyond intention.

What Pravakya Does

Pravakya creates meaningful platforms where people can learn, experience, express, develop and contribute. Our work takes many forms — learning programmes, cultural initiatives, youth development, community engagement, awareness activities and collaborative projects.

We work with educational institutions, corporates, community groups, artists, professionals, volunteers and social-impact partners. The foundation is intentionally flexible, designed to grow as new needs and opportunities emerge.

Vision

The Society We Work Towards

A society where knowledge inspires action, creativity finds expression, and every individual is empowered to contribute meaningfully to a better future.

Meet the founders guiding Pravakya Foundation — bringing together professional experience, creative practice and a shared commitment to meaningful social impact.

Founder
Founder & Visionary

Josith Shetty

Founder, Pravakya Foundation | Professional Experience in Public Speaking & Theatre-Led Learning

Josith Shetty founded Pravakya Foundation with a clear conviction: that learning becomes truly meaningful only when it moves beyond information and into action. Drawing on professional experience, a deep involvement in public speaking and a theatre-led approach to development, he brings together disciplines that rarely sit in the same room.

His vision for Pravakya is of a platform that grows with the people it works with — one that doesn't fix a single model but stays responsive, collaborative and honest about where it stands at every stage of its journey.

"Our aim is to create platforms where people can learn something meaningful, discover their potential, express themselves confidently and contribute to society."

Connect with Josith:
Co-Founder & Collaborator

Shrikantta Rao

Co-Founder, Pravakya Foundation | Educational Initiatives & Collaborative Programme Design

Shrikantta Rao co-founded Pravakya with a focus on making learning genuinely practical. His contribution to the foundation centres on the design and development of educational initiatives — programmes that give participants something they can actually use, reflect on and build from.

He has been closely involved in building the collaborative approach that defines Pravakya's work — ensuring that every programme is shaped not just by what the foundation wants to deliver, but by what participants actually need.

"We don't choose a student's path — we help them discover it. That's the philosophy behind every programme we build."
